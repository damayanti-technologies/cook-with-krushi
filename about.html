Our Story & Purpose

Mastering the Art of Homestyle Cooking

Cook With Krushi was born from a simple belief: anyone can cook authentic, soul-satisfying meals when guided with patience, precise proportions, and step-by-step clarity.

Our Five-Stage Philosophy

1. Discover → 2. Prepare → 3. Cook → 4. Complete → 5. Enjoy

Too many recipe sites overwhelm cooks with long ads, cluttered video auto-plays, and confusing instructions. Cook With Krushi removes that friction entirely. With our interactive ingredient preparation checklist, built-in cooking timers with audio notifications, and distraction-free cooking mode, you are empowered in the kitchen from start to finish.

A Tribute to Regional Flavors

From the slow-simmered aromatic spices of Hyderabadi Dum Biryani to the tangy comfort of Andhra Tomato Pappu and the festive sweetness of Sankranti Pongal, we honor authentic regional Telugu recipes alongside beloved Indian culinary classics. Full bilingual support in both English and Telugu ensures culinary heritage is celebrated and shared across generations.

Owned & Developed by Damayanti Technologies

Cook With Krushi is engineered and operated by Damayanti Technologies as a free, privacy-first culinary initiative. We believe your kitchen data belongs strictly to you. That's why your favorites, ingredient lists, and cooking milestones are saved directly on your device using IndexedDB, without mandatory accounts or intrusive tracking.
