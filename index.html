Chef's Wisdom

Cooking Tips of the Day

Little secrets that elevate homestyle cooking into restaurant perfection.

Biryani Dum Technique

Layering at 70% Cooked Rice

Never fully boil basmati rice before layering in Dum Biryani. Drain when the grain breaks into three pieces under your thumbnail.

Crispy Masala Dosa

The Sugar & Chana Dal Secret

Add 2 tbsp soaked chana dal and 1/2 tsp sugar to your batter before grinding. This creates the signature golden restaurant crust.

Tadka / Tempering

Mustard Seeds Must Crackle

Mustard seeds are bitter until fully crackled. Heat ghee until hot, add seeds, wait for them to pop before adding cumin and curry leaves.
