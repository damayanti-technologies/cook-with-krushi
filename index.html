Meet "Cook With Me" — Your Hands-Free Kitchen Voice Guide

Don't touch your screen with floury or oily hands! Cook With Me speaks every instruction aloud using natural speech synthesis and listens for voice commands like "Next step", "Start timer", and "Repeat" in both English and Telugu.

🎙️ Try Cook With Me Mode → View Kitchen Glossary
Hands-Free Voice Commands:
🗣️ "Next step" → Advances to next step & reads instruction
🗣️ "Repeat" / "మళ్లీ చెప్పు" → Speaks instruction again
🗣️ "Start timer" → Triggers countdown with zero drift
🗣️ "How much time is left?" → Assistant speaks remaining time
