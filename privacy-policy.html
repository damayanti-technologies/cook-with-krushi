Privacy First

Privacy Policy

At Cook With Krushi, owned and operated by Damayanti Technologies, your privacy is fundamental to our architecture. We designed this platform from the ground up so you never need an account, password, or third-party authentication.

1. Client-Side Storage Only

All your personal interactions—including saved recipes (favorites), in-progress cooking checkpoints, ingredient checklist completion, and cooking history logs—are saved solely inside your browser using standard browser APIs:

  • IndexedDB: Encrypted and sandboxed by your browser for favorites, in-progress cooking steps, and cooking journal records.
  • LocalStorage: Used exclusively for storing your language selection (English or Telugu) and sound preferences.

2. Zero External Tracking & Zero Databases

We do not transmit your recipe searches, saved dishes, or cooking times to any external backend server or database. We do not use third-party analytics trackers, advertising pixels, or cross-site fingerprinting scripts.

3. Data Portability & Deletion

Because all data resides in your browser, you retain total control over it. You can export a full JSON backup of your kitchen data or erase everything permanently at any time via the My Kitchen dashboard.

4. Contact

For any privacy inquiries, reach out to Damayanti Technologies at our contact page.
